One question, asked six different ways: how do you turn unstructured human signal into something a system can reason over?

I'm a lead ML engineer. For more than eight years I've built machine-learning systems for teams in the US and Europe: first speech and text models for healthcare, then document intelligence on OCR and layout models, then the MLOps and cloud work that keeps models running, and now agentic AI assistants for law enforcement.

Along the way I reached 0.92 field-level F1 extracting data from thousands of document templates, and 0.91 F1 classifying responses for a lead-generation product. What I care about most is what happens after the model works: retrieval, streaming, access control, evaluation and monitoring.

I'm from Shakargarh, in Pakistan's Punjab. I finished matriculation at The Educators Higher Secondary School Shakargarh, then F.Sc in Pre-Engineering at Govt. Degree College, Zafarwal, before moving to Islamabad to study computer science at FAST-NUCES.

2023 to 2025

Understand

HighPlains Computing, Los Angeles (remote)

Lead ML Ops Engineer

Two products, one lesson: unstructured data gets valuable once you can ask it questions.

Hercule

  • An analytics platform for law enforcement that takes in audio, video, images and PDFs and turns them into structured, queryable data.
  • Self-hosted Whisper handles speech-to-text, a Qwen vision-language model reads documents and images, and AWS Textract extracts structured fields.
  • The extracted text feeds a RAG system on ChromaDB with AWS Bedrock embeddings, queried through a LangGraph chatbot.

Lili

  • A healthcare solution that reads physician notes, extracts ICD-10 and CPT codes, medications and anatomy, and automates entering the encounter in an EHR.
  • Ran on AWS Batch, SageMaker, ECS, Athena and DynamoDB, and talked to the EHR through FHIR APIs for encounters, appointments and other clinical operations.

Careem

  • Delivered usage and login analytics reports for the ride-hailing client.

Whisper, Qwen VLM, AWS Textract, ChromaDB, AWS Bedrock, LangGraph, FHIR, SageMaker

2025 to now

Act

TargetZero, USA (remote)

Sr. AI Consultant

From answering questions to doing the work, safely.

  • Architected and deployed Zero, a multi-agent AI assistant that lets law-enforcement officers and managers query incident data in plain language, built on Pydantic AI, FastAPI and AWS Bedrock (Claude).
  • Built the streaming layer on Redis Streams and Server-Sent Events so long agent sessions survive dropped connections without paying for inference twice.
  • Designed context management that keeps long conversations coherent while holding token cost down.
  • Implemented multi-tenant access control and prompt-injection guardrails for a sensitive environment.
  • Built an evaluation framework that pairs automated regression checks with LLM-based grading, plus audit logging, cost tracking and tracing.
  • Added RAG over incident narratives with LanceDB and Bedrock Titan embeddings, and connectors that pull data from CAD, jail-management and law-enforcement records systems.

Pydantic AI, AWS Bedrock, FastAPI, Redis Streams, LanceDB, PostgreSQL
